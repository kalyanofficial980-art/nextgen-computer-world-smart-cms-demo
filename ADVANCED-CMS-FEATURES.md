# Advanced CMS Release Scope

## Branding
Logo upload, alternate footer logo, favicon, hero image, business name, tagline, website description, six website colours and announcement bar.

## Business settings
Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map, working hours, GST/registration references, service areas and social links.

## Catalogue and inventory
Products, SKU, quantity, cost price, regular price, timed offer price, condition, stock status, warranty, primary image, gallery, specs, included items, tags, SEO, featured, best seller, new arrival, archive and restore.

## Marketing
Offers, coupon codes, artwork, publish windows, reviews, ratings, review images, verification status, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, uploaded videos, thumbnails and homepage controls.

## CRM and sales
Customers, marketing consent, private notes, sales history, invoice references, payment status, warranty dates, public-safe sold notes, automatic inventory adjustment and CSV export.

## Website content
Homepage section titles/order/visibility, FAQs, privacy, terms, refund, warranty and delivery policies.

## Operations and security
Admin-only RLS, storage policies, server-only enquiry submission, rate limiting, activity logs, health endpoint, security headers, CI checks and production smoke-test support.
