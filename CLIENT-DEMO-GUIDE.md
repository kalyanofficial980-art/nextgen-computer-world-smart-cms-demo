# Client Demo Guide

## Public website walkthrough

1. Open the homepage and show the business-controlled logo, colours, hero content and announcement bar.
2. Open the catalogue and demonstrate product search, filters, stock, offer pricing and comparison.
3. Open a product to show the image gallery, specifications, quantity, warranty and WhatsApp enquiry.
4. Open Offers, Reviews and Videos & Reels.
5. Show Contact and the editable legal-policy pages.

## Owner CMS walkthrough

1. Sign in through `/admin/login`.
2. Overview: products, enquiries, offers, reviews, customers, sales and paid-sales value.
3. Branding: upload logo/favicon/hero and change website colours.
4. Business: update phone, WhatsApp, email, address, hours and social profiles.
5. Products: upload the main image and gallery, manage SKU, stock, pricing, SEO and archive/restore.
6. Offers: add artwork, dates, coupon and homepage visibility.
7. Reviews: add rating, customer permission status and optional image.
8. Videos: add YouTube/Instagram/Facebook links or upload MP4/WebM.
9. Customers and Sales: create customer history, record a sale and show automatic stock adjustment.
10. Homepage, FAQs and Legal: enable/reorder sections and edit policies without changing code.
11. Activity log: show the latest CMS changes.

## Handoff notes

- Use real customer reviews only with permission.
- Never enter customer phone numbers or private details in public sales notes.
- Confirm product price, availability and warranty before sale.
- Have business-specific legal content reviewed professionally.
